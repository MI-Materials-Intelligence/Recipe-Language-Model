Compound: PEAI (Phenethylammonium iodide; C₈H₁₂IN)

1. Functional Overview  
Functional Structure of This Compound  
- Ionic composition: [C₆H₅–CH₂–CH₂–NH₃]⁺ · I⁻  
- Functional groups:  
  - **–NH₃⁺ (protonated primary amine):** forms ionic bonds and N–H···I hydrogen bonds with under-coordinated Pb²⁺ and halide vacancies, providing defect passivation.  
  - **Phenethyl (C₆H₅–CH₂–CH₂–):** hydrophobic aromatic chain that blocks H₂O/O₂ infiltration and stabilizes the surface under humid or thermal stress.  
  - **I⁻ (iodide):** fills halide vacancies, suppresses ion migration, and contributes to lattice healing.  
- Structural behavior: upon surface interaction, PEA⁺ induces the in-situ formation of a thin **Ruddlesden–Popper (RP) 2D PEA₂PbI₄** layer, generating an energy-level gradient and providing hydrophobic encapsulation.

2. Detailed Functional Analysis  
Function: As passivation  

Mechanism of Action:  
- **Defect passivation and surface healing:**  
  PEAI repairs both cationic (under-coordinated Pb²⁺) and anionic (V_I) surface defects through electrostatic and hydrogen bonding interactions, significantly lowering surface trap density and nonradiative recombination losses[1].  
- **Grain-boundary passivation and structural regulation:**  
  PEA⁺ tends to accumulate at grain boundaries, forming quasi-2D PEA₂PbI₄ domains that bridge between grains. This enhances crystal orientation and reduces recombination at GBs, while the hydrophobic aryl group protects against humidity-induced degradation[2].  
- **Interfacial modification and charge transport:**  
  A monolayer-scale PEAI treatment establishes an interfacial dipole and an energy-level gradient between the 2D and 3D regions, facilitating hole extraction and suppressing interfacial recombination. In all-inorganic CsPbI₃, PEAI reacts with excess PbI₂ to form PEA₂PbI₄ and PEA₂(CsPbI₃)PbI₄, both of which aid defect passivation and carrier transport.  
- **Stability enhancement:**  
  The hydrophobic, low-dimensional capping layer effectively inhibits ion migration, blocks environmental species, and stabilizes the perovskite black phase, leading to improved operational and shelf stability.

Impact on Device Performance:  
- **Voc:** significant improvement due to reduced nonradiative recombination.  
- **FF:** moderate increase via enhanced charge extraction and lower interfacial loss.  
- **Jsc:** generally unchanged or slightly increased.  
- **Stability:** markedly enhanced under humidity and continuous illumination; inhibits black-to-yellow transition in CsPbI₃.

Key Characterization Evidence:  
XRD:  
- Appearance of **low-angle RP peaks (≈4–6° 2θ)** indicating formation of 2D PEA₂PbI₄ layers on the surface.  
- Main 3D perovskite reflections preserved; improved peak sharpness (reduced FWHM) showing enhanced crystallinity and orientation.  
- **PbI₂ (≈12.6°) peak intensity decreased** due to reaction/passivation of residual PbI₂.  
- Absence of new impurity phases at optimal PEAI concentrations (≤4.5%), confirming formation of coherent 2D/3D interfaces.
