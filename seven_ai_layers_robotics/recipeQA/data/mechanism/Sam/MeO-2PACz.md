# Compound: MeO-2PACz  
*([2-(3,6-dimethoxy-9H-carbazol-9-yl)ethyl]phosphonic acid)*

---

## 1. Chemical Structure

- **Common name:** MeO-2PACz  
- **Formal name:** [2-(3,6-dimethoxy-9H-carbazol-9-yl)ethyl]phosphonic acid  
- **Core architecture:**  
  A 3,6-dimethoxy-substituted carbazole π-core N-linked at the 9-position to a two-carbon alkyl chain, terminating in a phosphonic acid headgroup (–PO(OH)₂).
- **Structural description:**  
  The molecule is a carbazole π-system tethered to an oxide-compatible anchoring group via a short C₂ spacer, forming a classic phosphonate-based self-assembled monolayer (SAM) motif.

---

## 2. Functional Groups and Structural Characteristics

### 2.1 Phosphonic Acid Headgroup (–PO(OH)₂)
- **Anchoring behavior:**  
  Chemisorbs strongly to –OH-bearing oxide surfaces such as ITO and SnO₂.
- **Bonding mode:**  
  Forms robust P–O–M bonds (M = In, Sn), ensuring covalent attachment of the SAM.
- **Interfacial role:**  
  Provides stable, ultrathin anchoring without bulk incorporation into the oxide lattice[1].

### 2.2 3,6-Dimethoxy-Carbazole π-Core
- **Electronic function:**  
  Supplies the highest-occupied molecular orbital (HOMO), defining the ionization energy of the SAM.
- **Dipole generation:**  
  The electron-donating methoxy substituents enhance the interfacial dipole, influencing energy-level alignment at the buried interface.
- **π-System role:**  
  The conjugated carbazole core enables lateral π–π interactions within the monolayer, supporting ordered molecular packing[2].

### 2.3 Alkyl Tether (C₂ Spacer)
- **Conformational flexibility:**  
  Provides rotational freedom for optimal surface packing.
- **Electronic decoupling:**  
  Spatially separates the carbazole frontier orbitals from the oxide surface, reducing unwanted electronic hybridization while preserving dipole transmission[3].

---

## 3. Assembly and Interfacial Behavior

- **SAM formation:**  
  MeO-2PACz self-assembles into an ultrathin monolayer on oxide electrodes through phosphonate anchoring.
- **Orientation:**  
  The carbazole π-core is positioned above the oxide surface, while the phosphonic acid head remains bound to the substrate.
- **Packing:**  
  Methoxy substitution and the short linker promote dense, ordered coverage suitable for buried interfaces[4].

---

## 4. Functional Role at Oxide Interfaces

- **Energy-level alignment:**  
  The HOMO of the carbazole core and the induced interfacial dipole define the effective ionization energy at the oxide/perovskite interface.
- **Charge-selective behavior:**  
  Acts as a hole-selective contact by facilitating hole extraction while suppressing interfacial recombination.
- **Interface cleanliness:**  
  Provides a molecularly thin, well-defined interface without introducing bulk transport resistance.

---

## 5. Design Significance

- MeO-2PACz exemplifies a **carbazole–phosphonate SAM design** in which:
  - The **π-core** controls energetics and dipole formation.
  - The **phosphonic acid anchor** ensures robust oxide attachment.
  - The **short alkyl spacer** balances packing density, dipole transmission, and electronic decoupling.
- This design logic underpins its effectiveness as a buried-interface modifier in oxide-contact perovskite solar cells.
