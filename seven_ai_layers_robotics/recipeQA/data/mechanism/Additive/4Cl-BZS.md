# Compound: 4-Chlorobenzenesulfonate  
*(4Cl-BZS; Molecular formula: C₆H₄ClSO₃Na)*

---

## 1. Chemical Structure

- **Full name:** 4-chlorobenzenesulfonate  
- **Molecular formula:** C₆H₄ClSO₃Na  
- **Structural description:**  
  A para-substituted benzene ring bearing a chlorine atom (–Cl) and a sulfonate group (–SO₃⁻).  
- **Molecular characteristics:**  
  The molecule is rigid, planar, and approximately 5 Å in length.

---

## 2. Functional Groups and Structural Features

### 2.1 Sulfonate Group (–SO₃⁻)
- **Type:** Strong electron-withdrawing group, present as a fully deprotonated sulfonate anion.  
- **Properties:**  
  - Highly polar and significantly enhances water solubility.  
  - Influences electron distribution and hydrogen-bonding capacity when attached to an aromatic ring.  
- **Structural role:**  
  Contributes a strong negative charge and a major component of the molecular dipole.

### 2.2 Chlorine Substituent (–Cl)
- **Type:** Electron-withdrawing group via inductive effect (–I > +M), characteristic of an aryl chloride.  
- **Properties:**  
  - Deactivates the benzene ring toward electrophilic substitution.  
  - When para to the sulfonate group, contributes to the overall molecular dipole.  
- **Structural role:**  
  Participates in intermolecular and interfacial interactions through its C–Cl bond dipole.

### 2.3 Para-Substituted Benzene Ring
- **Type:** Aromatic conjugated system with 1,4-disubstitution.  
- **Properties:**  
  - Para substitution enforces planarity and rigidity of the molecular backbone.  
  - π-electron delocalization is modulated by both –SO₃⁻ and –Cl substituents.  
- **Overall effect:**  
  Generates a net molecular dipole arising from the sulfonate anion and the C–Cl bond.

---

## 3. Defect Passivation Mechanism

- **Dual-site coordination:**  
  - The chlorine atom forms Pb–Cl bonds with under-coordinated Pb²⁺ ions.  
  - The sulfonate group coordinates simultaneously with adjacent Pb²⁺ through Pb–O–S interactions.[1]
- **Passivation outcome:**  
  These combined interactions enable strong defect passivation at both cationic and anionic vacancy sites on the perovskite surface.[2]

---

## 4. Interfacial Configuration and Electronic Effects

- **Surface configuration:**  
  4Cl-BZS adopts a flat-lying orientation on the perovskite surface, forming an ultrathin and highly ordered passivation layer without introducing additional interfacial resistance.
- **Interfacial dipole effect:**  
  The intrinsic molecular dipole downshifts the perovskite conduction band.
- **Energy-level alignment:**  
  Reduced energy offset between the perovskite and the C₆₀ layer enhances electron extraction efficiency.

---

## 5. Impact on Film Quality and Device Performance

- Improves perovskite film quality.  
- Suppresses non-radiative recombination.  
- Enhances charge extraction at the electron-transport interface.  
- Collectively contributes to boosted photovoltaic device performance.
