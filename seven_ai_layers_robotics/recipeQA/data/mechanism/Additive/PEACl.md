# Compound: Phenethylammonium Chloride  
*(PEACl; Molecular formula: C₈H₁₂ClN)*

---

## 1. Chemical Structure

- **Full name:** Phenethylammonium chloride  
- **Molecular formula:** C₈H₁₂ClN  
- **Ionic composition:**  
  Phenethylammonium cation (PEA⁺) paired with chloride anion (Cl⁻)
- **Cation structure:**  
  PEA⁺ consists of a phenyl ring linked through a flexible two-carbon alkyl spacer to a terminal primary ammonium group (–NH₃⁺).

---

## 2. Functional Groups and Structural Characteristics

### 2.1 Phenethylammonium Cation (C₆H₅CH₂CH₂NH₃⁺)
- **Type:** Aryl-primary ammonium ion.
- **Charge and conformation:**  
  - Positive charge localized on the terminal –NH₃⁺ group.  
  - The phenyl–ethylene–ammonium architecture is semi-rigid, allowing conformational adaptation at interfaces.
- **Hydrogen-bonding capability:**  
  - N–H protons act as strong hydrogen-bond donors.  
  - Forms N–H···Cl⁻ (or N–H···X⁻) hydrogen-bond networks that passivate halide vacancies and under-coordinated Pb²⁺ sites on perovskite surfaces.
- **Hydrophobic and stacking effects:**  
  - The phenyl ring enables π–π stacking.  
  - Provides hydrophobic shielding that suppresses moisture ingress and helps control layer orientation (n-value distribution) in quasi-2D/3D perovskites.[1][2]

### 2.2 Chloride Anion (Cl⁻)
- **Type:** Spherical halide anion.
- **Hydrogen-bonding role:**  
  - Strong hydrogen-bond acceptor, forming ion pairs with –NH₃⁺ and extended three-dimensional hydrogen-bond lattices.
- **Coordination behavior:**  
  - In precursor solutions, coordinates with Pb²⁺ or Sn²⁺ to form intermediate complexes such as [PbCl₆]⁴⁻ or [SnCl₆]⁴⁻, tuning colloid size and crystallization kinetics.
- **Film-level role:**  
  - After annealing, can segregate to grain boundaries or surfaces.  
  - Passivates chloride vacancies, reduces non-radiative recombination, and promotes preferred crystal orientation and smoother film morphology.[1][2]

---

## 3. Interfacial and Phase-Engineering Mechanism

- PEA⁺ intercalates at perovskite surfaces or grain boundaries, forming quasi-2D/3D heterostructures.
- Cl⁻ coordinates with under-coordinated Pb²⁺ and fills halide vacancies.
- These combined interactions passivate deep-level defects and suppress non-radiative recombination.
- The bulky PEA⁺ cation provides hydrophobic shielding and steric hindrance, reducing moisture ingress and inhibiting ion migration.
- Chloride-mediated intermediate regulation promotes smooth morphology and preferred vertical crystal orientation.[2][3]

---

## 4. Resulting Film and Device-Level Effects

- Enlarged grain size and improved film uniformity.  
- Stabilization of the black α-phase.  
- Reduced J–V hysteresis.  
- Enhanced device efficiency and improved operational stability.
