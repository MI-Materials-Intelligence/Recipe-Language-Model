# Compound: Propylammonium Chloride  
*(PACl; Molecular formula: C₃H₁₀ClN)*

---

## 1. Chemical Structure

- **Full name:** Propylammonium chloride  
- **Molecular formula:** C₃H₁₀ClN  
- **Condensed formula:** CH₃CH₂CH₂NH₃Cl  
- **Ionic composition:**  
  Propylammonium cation (PA⁺) paired with chloride anion (Cl⁻)
- **Structural features:**  
  A short-chain alkylammonium salt in which the PA⁺ cation can readily deprotonate and volatilize, while Cl⁻ remains available for coordination with metal cations.

---

## 2. Functional Groups and Structural Characteristics

### 2.1 Propylammonium Cation (n-Pr–NH₃⁺)
- **Charge distribution:**  
  Localized positive charge on the –NH₃⁺ group.
- **Conformational properties:**  
  Non-conjugated alkyl chain with high rotational flexibility.
- **Hydrogen-bonding capability:**  
  Forms strong N–H···X (X = Cl⁻, Br⁻, I⁻) hydrogen-bond networks that passivate surface halide vacancies.
- **Spatial and interfacial role:**  
  - Small molecular footprint enables insertion at grain boundaries or within quasi-2D interlayers.  
  - Moderate hydrophobicity reduces moisture uptake while preserving solution processability.

### 2.2 Chloride Anion (Cl⁻)
- **Type:**  
  Spherical halide anion.
- **Coordination behavior:**  
  - Coordinates with Pb²⁺ or Sn²⁺ to form intermediate complexes such as [MCl₆]⁴⁻ in precursor solutions.  
  - In thin films, may reside at grain boundaries or partially incorporate into the lattice, influencing crystal orientation and morphology.

---

## 3. Crystallization and Defect-Passivation Mechanism

- **Early-stage passivation:**  
  The PA+ ions incorporated by top-down engineering can adsorb on the specific crystal facet of perovskite to form a growth template and reduce the phase transition energy barrier. PA⁺ participates in transient N–H···Cl⁻ hydrogen-bond networks that passivate surface defects during initial crystallization.[1][2]
- **Volatile growth-directing role:**  
  The propylammonium cation readily deprotonates and volatilizes upon modest heating, preventing long-term incorporation into the lattice.
- **Halide-mediated crystallization control:**  
  The presence of Cl− not only repaired deep trap states, which were generated by I vacancies of the perovskite films but also effectively passivated I vacancy defects in the perovskite surface.[3]

---

## 4. Film Morphology and Structural Outcomes

- Produces dense, pinhole-free perovskite films.  
- Increases grain size and improves crystal texture.  
- Reduces trap density and prolongs carrier lifetime.  
- Biases crystallization toward face-on (100) stacking.

---

## 5. Functional Summary

PACl functions as a **volatile growth director**:  
- Provides hydrogen-bonding and coordination-based defect passivation at early stages.  
- Directs crystallization toward a well-textured 3D perovskite phase.  
- Subsequently evaporates, leaving a phase-pure film with improved charge transport and enhanced open-circuit voltage.
