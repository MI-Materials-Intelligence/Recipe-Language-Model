# Compound: 1-(Phenylsulfonyl)pyrrole  
*(PSP; Molecular formula: C₁₀H₉NO₂S)*

---

## 1. Chemical Structure

- **Full name:** 1-(phenylsulfonyl)pyrrole  
- **Molecular formula:** C₁₀H₉NO₂S  
- **Structural features:**  
  A rigid aromatic heterocycle (pyrrole) conjugated to an electron-withdrawing phenyl-sulfonyl (–SO₂–Ph) moiety.  
  The molecule adopts a planar conformation. Sulfone oxygen atoms can form hydrogen bonds or coordinate with perovskite surfaces, while the pyrrolic nitrogen provides an additional hydrogen-bond donor site.

---

## 2. Functional Groups and Structural Characteristics

### 2.1 Pyrrole Ring
- **Type:** Aromatic five-membered heterocycle with one sp²-hybridized nitrogen.
- **Properties:**  
  - Rigid and planar.  
  - The N–H group is a strong hydrogen-bond donor (δ⁺ H).  
  - Upon deprotonation, the nitrogen can act as a ligand or hydrogen-bond acceptor.  
  - The π-system conjugates with the adjacent sulfonyl group, lowering electron density on the ring.

### 2.2 Phenyl-Sulfonyl Moiety (–SO₂–Ph)
- **Type:** Aryl sulfone (R–SO₂–R′).
- **Properties:**  
  - Strongly electron-withdrawing through both inductive (–I) and resonance (–M) effects.  
  - The two sulfone oxygen atoms carry partial negative charges (δ⁻ O), acting as bidentate hydrogen-bond acceptors or Lewis-basic coordination sites for metal ions.  
  - The phenyl ring contributes additional π–π stacking capability.

### 2.3 Combined Planar Conjugated System
- **Geometry and interactions:**  
  - Direct conjugation between the pyrrole and phenyl-sulfonyl group enforces a rigid, planar geometry (~5 Å length scale).  
  - Facilitates strong surface adsorption via cooperative hydrogen bonding (N–H···O, C–H···O) and metal coordination (O···Pb²⁺/Sn²⁺).

---

## 3. Defect Passivation and Crystallization Mechanism

- **Metal-site coordination:**  
  Sulfone oxygen atoms coordinate under-coordinated Pb²⁺ (or Sn²⁺) ions, passivating surface metal vacancies.
- **Halide interaction:**  
  The pyrrolic N–H functions as a potent hydrogen-bond donor, interacting with surface halides or carbonyl-type defects.
- **Crystallization modulation:**  
  PSP transiently moderates crystallization, mitigating vertical FA⁺/Cs⁺ segregation in FA–Cs perovskite films.[1]

---

## 4. Interfacial Ordering and Film Stability

- **Ordered adsorption:**  
  The extended π-system of the pyrrole–sulfonyl unit, together with the appended phenyl ring, promotes π–π stacking and ordered surface adsorption.
- **Coverage and cohesion:**  
  Enhanced intermolecular cohesion yields uniform surface coverage.
- **Stability outcome:**  
  These effects collectively improve perovskite film stability through effective surface passivation and ordered interfacial assembly.
