# Compound: Meta-Methoxy-Phenethylammonium Chloride  
*(m-MeO-PEACl; Molecular formula: C₉H₁₄ClNO)*

---

## 1. Chemical Structure

- **Full name:** Meta-methoxy-phenethylammonium chloride  
- **Molecular formula:** C₉H₁₄ClNO  
- **Ionic form:**  
  [m-MeO–C₆H₄–CH₂CH₂–NH₃]⁺ · Cl⁻
- **Structural features:**  
  A phenethylammonium scaffold bearing a methoxy group (–OCH₃) at the meta (3-) position of the phenyl ring.  
  The methoxy substituent adopts a bent conformation.

---

## 2. Functional Groups and Structural Characteristics

### 2.1 Meta-Methoxy Substituent (–OCH₃)
- **Electronic properties:**  
  - Moderate electron-donating (+M) effect increases the π-electron density of the phenyl ring.  
- **Functional roles:**  
  - Strengthens cation–π interactions with under-coordinated Pb²⁺ sites.  
  - The lone pair on the ether oxygen introduces an additional hydrogen-bonding site.  
  - Can anchor surface water molecules or interact with halide vacancies.

### 2.2 Phenyl Ring
- **Structural role:**  
  - Rigid and planar aromatic backbone.  
- **Interfacial interactions:**  
  - Enables π–π stacking to cover grain boundaries.  
  - Cooperates with the methoxy group to passivate electron-deficient surface atoms and reduce defect density.

### 2.3 Ethylene Linker (–CH₂CH₂–)
- **Function:**  
  - Provides conformational flexibility.  
  - Allows the –NH₃⁺ headgroup to dock into the perovskite cage while the methoxy-aryl segment points outward.  
  - Creates a hydrophilic-to-hydrophobic gradient at the interface.

### 2.4 Protonated Ammonium with Chloride Counter-Ion (–NH₃⁺ · Cl⁻)
- **Hydrogen-bond network:**  
  - The ammonium group forms N–H···X hydrogen bonds with surface halide vacancies, lowering trap-state density.
- **Halide role:**  
  - Cl⁻ can migrate to fill iodide or bromide vacancies, suppressing non-radiative recombination.  
- **Solubility and exchange:**  
  - The ionic nature provides high water solubility and cation-exchange capability.

---

## 3. Defect Passivation and Interfacial Mechanism

- Electron donation from the methoxy group enhances cation–π interactions with under-coordinated Pb²⁺.  
- Hydrogen bonding from –NH₃⁺ and the ether oxygen stabilizes surface halide vacancies and polar sites.  
- Cl⁻ contributes halide-vacancy healing, jointly reducing trap-state density.

---

## 4. Crystallization and Phase Behavior

- m-MeO-PEACl promotes vertically oriented (001) crystal growth.  
- Improves phase purity of the perovskite film.  
- Maintains an unchanged band gap while optimizing crystallization kinetics.

---

## 5. Resulting Film and Device-Level Effects

- Reduced defect density at surfaces and grain boundaries.  
- Improved crystallinity and phase stability.  
- Enhanced overall performance and stability of perovskite films.
