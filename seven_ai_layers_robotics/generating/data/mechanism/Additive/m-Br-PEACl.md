# Compound: Meta-Bromo-Phenethylammonium Chloride  
*(m-Br-PEACl; Molecular formula: C₈H₁₁BrClN)*

---

## 1. Chemical Structure

- **Full name:** Meta-bromo-phenethylammonium chloride  
- **Molecular formula:** C₈H₁₁BrClN  
- **Ionic form:**  
  [m-Br–C₆H₄–CH₂CH₂–NH₃]⁺ · Cl⁻
- **Structural features:**  
  The molecule consists of a phenethylammonium cation with a bromine substituent at the meta (3-) position of the phenyl ring, paired with a chloride counter-anion.

---

## 2. Functional Groups and Structural Characteristics

### 2.1 Meta-Bromine Substituent (m-Br)
- **Electronic properties:**  
  Strongly electron-withdrawing and heavy, enhancing molecular polarizability and introducing a heavy-atom effect.
- **Structural role:**  
  Modulates π-electron density of the phenyl ring and participates in non-covalent Br···X interactions that influence crystal orientation and interfacial dipole formation.

### 2.2 Phenethylammonium Moiety (–CH₂CH₂NH₃⁺)
- **Cationic functionality:**  
  The –NH₃⁺ group acts as a hydrogen-bond donor and provides electrostatic interaction with negatively charged defects.
- **Backbone role:**  
  The phenethyl scaffold enables π–π stacking and offers hydrophobic shielding at the perovskite interface.

### 2.3 Chloride Anion (Cl⁻)
- **Halide-passivation site:**  
  Serves as a hydrogen-bond acceptor and provides an additional halide source for defect passivation.
- **Interfacial role:**  
  Cooperates with the ammonium group to balance charge and stabilize grain boundaries.

---

## 3. Dimensionality and Phase Engineering

- In situ incorporation of m-Br-PEACl into the 3D perovskite lattice generates n = 2–4 Ruddlesden–Popper quasi-2D phases.
- Forms a 3D/2D gradient heterojunction that significantly enhances moisture and thermal stability.[1]

---

## 4. Dual Defect Passivation Mechanism

- **Halide-vacancy passivation:**  
  Br⁻ preferentially occupies halide vacancies (Vₓ).
- **Cationic defect passivation:**  
  Cl⁻ further passivates Pb²⁺ dangling bonds at grain boundaries.
- **Net effect:**  
  Cooperative Br⁻/Cl⁻ passivation lowers trap-state density by 1–2 orders of magnitude.

---

## 5. Morphology and Crystallization Kinetics

- The m-Br-PEA⁺ cation retards crystal growth, promoting large, vertically oriented grains.
- Reduces surface RMS roughness.
- Formation of a pre-anneal intermediate phase, (PEA)₂PbBr₂Cl₂, lowers the nucleation barrier.
- Suppresses δ-phase impurities and increases overall phase purity.

---

## 6. Integrated Mechanism and Film-Level Effects

- π–π stacking and hydrophobic shielding from the phenethyl backbone improve interfacial robustness.
- Meta-bromine substitution lowers π-electron density and induces an interfacial dipole.
- Cooperative hydrogen-bonding between –NH₃⁺ and Cl⁻ passivates surface and grain-boundary defects.
- Collectively enhances film crystallinity, phase stability, and device performance.
