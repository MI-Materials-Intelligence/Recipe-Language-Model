# Compound: 1,3-bis(cyanomethyl)imidazolium Chloride  
*(BcmimCl; Molecular formula: C₇H₇ClN₄)*

---

## 1. Chemical Structure

- **Full name:** 1,3-bis(cyanomethyl) imidazolium chloride  
- **Molecular formula:** C₇H₇ClN₄  
- **Ionic form:**  
  [Bcmim]⁺ · Cl⁻
- **Structural features:**  
  The molecule consists of a planar imidazolium core bearing substituents at the N1 and N3 positions, forming a rigid, positively charged cation. The salt is thermally stable above 250 °C and capable of forming ion pairs or coordinating to Pb²⁺.

---

## 2. Functional Groups and Structural Features

### 2.1 Overall Cationic Nature ([Bcmim]⁺)
- **Charge and geometry:**  
  Positively charged, rigid, and planar imidazolium cation.
- **Interaction capability:**  
  Enables electrostatic (ion-pairing), hydrogen-bonding, and coordination interactions with anions or metal ions such as Pb²⁺.
- **Imidazolium core:**  
  Contains an acidic C2–H proton that can function as a hydrogen-bond donor.

### 2.2 Nitrile Termini (–C≡N)
- **Type:**  
  Lewis-basic nitrogen donors.
- **Properties:**  
  Provide coordination ability toward under-coordinated Pb²⁺ ions, particularly in precursor or intermediate states during perovskite film formation.

### 2.3 Chloride Counter-Anion (Cl⁻)
- **Type:**  
  Spherical halide anion.
- **Properties:**  
  - Strong hydrogen-bond acceptor, especially toward C2–H and other ring C–H protons.  
  - Readily forms ion pairs with the [Bcmim]⁺ cation.  
  - Capable of directly coordinating to Pb²⁺ or bridging between Pb²⁺ centers in perovskite lattices.

---

## 3. Defect Passivation and Coordination Mechanism

- **Lewis-base coordination:**  
  The nitrile groups on the [Bcmim]⁺ cation coordinate with under-coordinated Pb²⁺ ions, passivating metal-related defects in precursor and intermediate phases.
- **Halide-vacancy filling:**  
  The Cl⁻ anion directly occupies halide vacancies, participating actively in the halide chemistry of the perovskite film.
- **Hydrogen-bonding network:**  
  Strong hydrogen-bonding interactions between Cl⁻ and the imidazolium C–H sites stabilize ion pairs and interfacial structures.[1]

---

## 4. Role in Perovskite Film Formation

- Suppresses detrimental intermediate phases during processing.  
- Promotes grain enlargement and compositional homogenization.  
- Passivates defects through combined cationic coordination and halide compensation.

---

## 5. Resulting Film and Device-Level Effects

- Enables the formation of high-quality perovskite films with reduced defect density.  
- Improves film uniformity and structural integrity.  
- Contributes to enhanced optoelectronic properties and improved device performance.

---

## 6. Design Implication

- The precise Lewis-basic ionic-liquid design of BcmimCl, combining a rigid imidazolium cation with coordinating nitrile groups and a strongly interacting Cl⁻ anion, underpins its superior performance compared with other imidazolium-based ionic liquids in perovskite processing.
