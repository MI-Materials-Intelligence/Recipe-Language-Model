Compound: PEABr (Phenethylammonium bromide; C₈H₁₂BrN)

1. Functional Overview  
Functional Structure of This Compound  
- Ionic composition: [C₆H₅–CH₂–CH₂–NH₃]⁺ · Br⁻  
- Functional groups:  
  - **–NH₃⁺ (protonated primary amine):** forms ionic and hydrogen-bonding interactions (N–H···X, X = I⁻/Br⁻) with under-coordinated Pb²⁺ or halide vacancies, passivating deep-level defects.  
  - **Phenyl–ethyl chain:** hydrophobic, sterically bulky spacer that creates a physical barrier to moisture and oxygen ingress; supports π–π stacking to drive formation of ordered interfacial layers.  
  - **Br⁻ (bromide):** fills halide vacancies (I⁻ or Br⁻), reduces trap density, and forms stable N–H···Br hydrogen-bond networks; trace Br⁻ diffusion tunes lattice strain and slightly adjusts bandgap.  
- Structural behavior: PEABr spontaneously forms a thin **2D PEA₂Pb(BrxI1-x)₄ (or mixed PEA₂PbX₄)** layer atop the 3D perovskite during post-treatment or annealing, yielding a 2D/3D heterojunction that passivates defects, optimizes energy alignment, and enhances stability.

2. Detailed Functional Analysis  
Function: As passivation  

Mechanism of Action: 
- **Defect filling and lattice healing:**  
  –NH₃⁺ electrostatically binds to Pb²⁺ and halide vacancies, while Br⁻ compensates anionic defects by partially substituting I⁻ or occupying vacant sites. This dual action reduces trap density and improves lattice order[1].  
- **2D/3D interface engineering:**  
  PEABr reacts with surface PbX₂ species to form an ultrathin 2D PEA₂Pb(BrxI1-x)₄ (or mixed-halide) overlayer. This layer serves as a defect-passivating interface, suppressing interfacial recombination and facilitating hole extraction to the HTL. The 2D/3D band alignment reduces interfacial energy barriers, leading to enhanced Voc and FF[2].  
- **Crystallization control:**  
  During film formation, PEA⁺ modulates nucleation kinetics, slowing crystal growth and producing larger, well-oriented grains with fewer pinholes and reduced boundary density. The improved film uniformity supports more efficient carrier transport.  
- **Ion-migration suppression:**  
  The dense 2D capping layer combined with Br⁻ back-filling and –NH₃⁺ anchoring inhibits halide and Pb²⁺ ion migration, stabilizing device performance under prolonged bias or illumination.  
- **Stability enhancement:**  
  The hydrophobic phenyl-ethyl backbone provides an effective moisture and oxygen barrier. The resulting 2D overlayer shields the underlying lattice, significantly improving environmental and thermal stability.

Impact on Device Performance:  
- **Voc:** significantly increased due to reduced nonradiative recombination and improved energy-level alignment.  
- **FF:** enhanced through suppressed interfacial recombination and optimized charge extraction.  
- **Jsc:** maintained or slightly improved owing to efficient carrier collection and reduced trap-assisted loss.  
- **Stability:** greatly improved humidity, light, and thermal tolerance via the hydrophobic 2D protective layer and ion-migration suppression.

Key Characterization Evidence:  
XRD:  
- **Emergence of low-angle reflections (~4–6° 2θ)** corresponding to formation of the 2D PEA₂Pb(BrxI1-x)₄ (or mixed PEA₂PbX₄) layer.  
- **Main 3D perovskite peaks** retained with **FWHM narrowing** of (100)/(110) reflections, indicating improved crystallinity and orientation.  
- **PbI₂ (≈12.6°)** peak intensity **decreased**, consistent with the reaction of surface PbI₂ with PEABr and elimination of excess PbX₂.  
- Absence of new impurity phases confirms coherent 2D/3D heterointerface formation and stable film morphology.
