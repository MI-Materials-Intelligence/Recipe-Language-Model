# Compound: meta-Bromo-Phenylethylammonium Iodide  
*(m-Br-PEAI; Molecular formula: C₈H₁₁BrIN)*

---

## 1. Chemical Structure

- **English name:** meta-bromo-phenylethylammonium iodide  
- **Common abbreviation:** m-Br-PEAI  
- **Molecular formula:** C₈H₁₁BrIN  
- **Ionic form:**  
  [3-Br–C₆H₄–CH₂CH₂–NH₃]⁺ · I⁻

---

## 2. Functional Groups and Structural Features

- **Protonated primary amine (–NH₃⁺):**  
  - Electrostatic anchoring to Pb²⁺ sites and halide vacancies.  
  - Forms an N–H···I–Pb hydrogen-bond network that passivates defects.  
  - Introduces a surface dipole that tunes the work function and increases Voc.

- **Meta-bromine substituent (m-Br):**  
  - Electron-withdrawing Br enhances π–π stacking, leading to a dense 2D overlayer.  
  - Enables weak Br···I halogen bonding, cooperatively suppressing iodide migration and reducing hysteresis.

- **Phenyl–ethyl chain:**  
  - Provides a hydrophobic barrier that blocks H₂O and O₂, improving damp-heat stability.  
  - Flexible linker relieves thermal stress and reduces crack formation.

- **Iodide anion (I⁻):**  
  - Directly fills iodide vacancies, eliminating deep-level traps.  
  - Forms N–H···I hydrogen bonds with –NH₃⁺, synergistically stabilizing the lattice.

- **Overall structural functionality:**  
  The combination of a meta-brominated phenyl ring and iodide counter-anion transforms the phenethylammonium scaffold into a multifunctional passivator that integrates defect healing, lattice stabilization, moisture resistance, and interfacial dipole control.

---

## 3. Dual-Site Defect Passivation

- **Cationic and anionic defect passivation:**  
  - –NH₃⁺ electrostatic anchoring and N–H···I–Pb hydrogen bonding passivate Pb²⁺ and I⁻ vacancies.  
  - Meta-Br introduces additional Br···I halogen bonds that further lock defect sites.[1]

---

## 4. 2D/3D Interface Engineering

- The electron-deficient phenyl ring promotes π–π stacking.  
- An in-situ dense PEA₂PbI₄ 2D layer forms at the surface.  
- The resulting energy-level gradient reduces interfacial recombination, enhancing Voc and FF.[1]

---

## 5. Ion-Migration Suppression

- A synergistic hydrogen-bond and halogen-bond dual network pins both Pb²⁺ and I⁻ ions.  
- This interaction markedly reduces ion migration and hysteresis.

---

## 6. Crystallization and Morphology Optimization

- m-Br-PEAI slows perovskite nucleation.  
- Induces large-grain, pinhole-free films with fewer grain boundaries.  
- Reduced grain-boundary density lowers non-radiative recombination losses.
