Compound: PDADI (1,3-diaminopropane dihydroiodide; C₃H₁₂I₂N₂)

1. Functional Overview  
Functional Structure of This Compound  
- Short-chain diammonium salt comprising a C3 linker with two terminal protonated primary amines (–NH₃⁺ … (CH₂)₃ … –NH₃⁺) balanced by two I⁻ counter-anions.  
- Dual –NH₃⁺ enables **dual-end anchoring** to defects (under-coordinated Pb²⁺, sites adjacent to V_I), while I⁻ locally compensates halide vacancies.  
- Acts as an organic spacer capable of forming **Dion–Jacobson (DJ)–type** local motifs; short interlayer spacing promotes stronger interlayer coupling vs bulky mono-ammoniums.

2. Detailed Functional Analysis  
Function: As passivation  

Mechanism of Action:  
- **Dual-end chemical passivation (“molecular sutures”)**: Each –NH₃⁺ binds independently to defect sites (e.g., Pb²⁺ antisites/under-coordination, edges around V_I), stitching grain boundaries and surfaces; I⁻ fills nearby V_I and assists local lattice reconstruction[1].  
- **Embedded/mixed-dimensional passivation**: Rather than forming a thick 2D cap, PDADI preferentially embeds at surfaces/GBs to create localized DJ-like regions that passivate both near-surface bulk and interfaces simultaneously[2].  
- **Defect chemistry refinement**: Consumes residual PbX₂ and reduces deep/shallow trap densities, suppressing non-radiative recombination and halide/ion migration pathways.  
- **Energy-level & dipole modulation**: The diammonium induces a surface dipole; at perovskite/ETL (e.g., C₆₀) it can yield **type-II alignment**, facilitating electron extraction and providing **field-effect passivation** (repelling minority carriers at the interface).  
- **Crystallization control & strain relief**: Post/soft treatment moderates intermediate formation, enlarges grains, smooths morphology, and relaxes GB strain—further reducing recombination.

Impact on Device Performance:  
- **Voc**: primary gain (defect/passivation + interfacial recombination suppression).  
- **FF**: improved via better charge extraction (type-II alignment, reduced series/transport loss) and lower trap-assisted recombination.  
- **Jsc**: neutral to slight increase (thinner/more conductive interfacial layer avoids optical penalties).  
- **Stability**: notable improvement (suppressed ion migration; chemically robust GBs; reduced moisture/thermal degradation at interfaces).

Key Characterization Evidence:  
XRD:  
- Main 3D perovskite reflections retained; **FWHM narrowing** of (100)/(110) consistent with improved crystallinity/strain relaxation.  
- **PbI₂ (≈12.6°)**: intensity typically **reduced** due to consumption/passivation of residual PbI₂.  
- **Low-angle features (≈4–6° 2θ, Cu Kα)**: weak/broad **DJ-like signatures** may appear at higher loadings or surface-rich regions; often subtle/absent at low passivation doses (consistent with embedded, mixed-dimensional motifs rather than a thick 2D overlayer).  
- No emergence of unwanted secondary phases when dosing is optimized.
