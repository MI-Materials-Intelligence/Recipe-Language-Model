# Compound: 3-(Methylthio)propylamine Hydroiodide  
*(3MTPAI; Molecular formula: C₄H₁₂INS)*

---

## 1. Chemical Structure

- **English name:** 3-(methylthio)propylamine hydroiodide  
- **Molecular formula:** C₄H₁₂INS  
- **Structural formula:**  
  CH₃–S–CH₂–CH₂–CH₂–NH₃⁺ I⁻  

---

## 2. Functional Groups and Structural Features

- **Methylthio group (–SCH₃):**  
  Provides a sulfur atom capable of forming coordinate bonds with Pb²⁺ on the perovskite surface, enabling chemical defect passivation.

- **Propyl chain (–CH₂CH₂CH₂–):**  
  Acts as a flexible alkyl linker that spatially separates the sulfur-containing group from the ammonium terminus.

- **Ammonium ion (–NH₃⁺):**  
  Positively charged; capable of forming hydrogen bonds or electrostatic interactions with negatively charged surface defects, such as iodide vacancies.

- **Overall structural functionality:**  
  The coexistence of a sulfur donor group and a protonated ammonium group enables simultaneous chemical passivation (via S–Pb coordination) and hydrogen-bond passivation (via N–H···I interactions) at the perovskite interface.

---

## 3. Related Chemical Properties

- Alkylammonium salt with weak acidity (pKₐ of the parent amine ≈ 9–10).  
- The methylthio group can form S→M coordinate bonds with metal ions (e.g., Pb²⁺) for surface passivation.  
- Reacts with strong bases to generate the free amine CH₃S(CH₂)₃NH₂.  
- Forms precipitates with soft acids such as Ag⁺ and Hg²⁺.  
- Sensitive to oxidants; under prolonged light exposure or elevated temperature, the methylthio group may be oxidized to sulfoxide or sulfone.

---

## 4. Defect Passivation Function

- **Chemical passivation of cationic defects:**  
  The –SCH₃ group forms S→Pb coordinate bonds, preferentially anchoring deep-level defects such as iodide vacancies and Pb clusters, thereby reducing the density of non-radiative recombination centers.

- **Hydrogen-bond passivation of anionic defects:**  
  The –NH₃⁺ terminus stabilizes surface iodide vacancies through N–H···I hydrogen bonding, suppresses ion migration, and enhances film crystallinity and compositional uniformity.[1][2]

---

## 5. Energy-Level and Interface Dipole Modulation

- 3MTPAI adopts a parallel orientation on the perovskite surface, introducing a downward interfacial dipole.  
- This dipole slightly elevates the valence-band edge and repels minority carriers (holes), thereby diminishing interface recombination.  
- When combined with the diammonium passivator PDAI₂, 3MTPAI provides chemical passivation while PDAI₂ contributes field-effect passivation.  
- The two passivators operate without mutual interference, realizing a **dual-molecule dual-passivation (DMDP)** strategy.[1]
